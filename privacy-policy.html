Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Aethrix automated clip uploader handles information. The App is operated by Keac (TheKeac), an individual based in Texas, United States.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information. It is a local desktop script that uploads video clips to TikTok on behalf of the account owner only. No user data is gathered, transmitted, or retained.

How the App Works

The App runs locally on a personal computer and uses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload pre-processed video clips to the @aethrixai TikTok account. No third parties have access to credentials or the upload pipeline.

Third-Party Services

The App interacts with TikTok's API. Please refer to TikTok's own Privacy Policy for information on how TikTok handles data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
